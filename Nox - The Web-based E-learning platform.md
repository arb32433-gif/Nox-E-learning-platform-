
Introduction

Nox is a e-learning platform built for enthusiasts to learn and share their knowledge. "Nox" meaning "Night" in Latin is a reference to the night sky, symbolizing the vastness of knowledge and the endless possibilities of learning. 
	
Structure 

1. User flow:
   - **Landing Page** (`Nox – Learn to run AI agents.html`): Welcomes users with interactive intro animations, module previews, and "Enter Nox" call-to-actions.
   - **Sign In Portal** (`Nox Portal – Sign in.html`): Serves as the authentication gate in between. Verifies credentials or continues existing session, saves session state to localStorage, and transitions with "Access granted" curtain.
   - **E-learning Dashboard** (`Nox – Agentic Orchestration e-learning (1).html`): The main learning environment with modular lessons, practice missions, progress tracking, and leaderboard. Links back to the landing page and sign-in portal.
   - **Progress & Analytics Dashboard** (`Nox – Your progress.html`): Visualized analytics, competency radar against cohort metrics, module filters, goal tracking, and direct lesson launcher.
   - **Learner Profile & Journey** (`Nox – Profile.html`): Identity hub displaying current title, level progress, XP milestones, earned badges, callsign customization, and activity heatmap.
   - **Study Notes & Vault** (`Nox – Notes.html`): Dedicated study notes workspace with markdown editing, preview, bidirectional wiki links, outline navigator, and direct lesson handoffs.
   - **Settings & Preferences** (`Nox – Settings.html`): Account management, theme selection, motion sensitivity, and learning reminders.
